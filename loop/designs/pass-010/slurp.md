# design-2: slurp

**Premise.** A tea tray where everything falls toward whatever is nearest and the light lies on the floor, crossed by one absurdly long looping straw through which a teacup-sized sleeper blows its dream; the camera follows the straw from the sleeper's side, once round the teapot, to the soap bubble on its far end.

## Ten premises

1. ~~**Storm in a teacup**: the camera circles up the inside of a stirred cup.~~ Struck: teacup plus spiral is the first join anyone makes.
2. ~~**Bubble bath**: foam mountains in a tub, the camera rises from the plughole.~~ Struck: soap film means bath.
3. ~~**Steam**: the camera is a wisp curling off a cup.~~ Struck: a rising helix over tea is steam.
4. ~~**Pocket planets**: teacup-sized planetoids, each with its own down.~~ Struck: the gravity law's stock picture, and floating islands.
5. ~~**Shadow theatre**: a toy stage with footlights, puppets hopping between shadows.~~ Struck: up-light plus shadows means footlights.
6. **The peel**: one unbroken spiral of lemon peel hanging in the air; the camera rides its inner curve.
7. **The snail**: a mite's climb round the whorl of a sleeping snail whose shell is a soap bubble.
8. **Dew line**: a single thread strung with drops, each holding the world upside down.
9. **Wire and film**: a bubble-blower's bent-wire world; the camera rides a soap-film helicoid.
10. **The long straw**: a sleeper blows a bubble through a straw with a loop in it, across a tea tray where tea falls up.

**Chosen: 10.** It is the only one where the rail's curve is an object the viewer recognises and smiles at (the loop of a crazy straw), and where the exit is a punchline (the bubble on the end of the straw is the sleeper's dream, and the dream is the next world). The peel and the snail are a shaft in disguise next door to the helix; the dew line and the wire world are handsome but nobody lives in them.

**Palette, named.** *Primrose* (pale cool lemon: the tea-light, HDR), *bottle green* (sky zenith and all shade), *celadon* (grey-green glaze, shaded porcelain, lace), *bone white* (lit porcelain, fur), *vermilion* (straw stripe, ear linings, cheeks, a rare painted band), *film sheen* (thin rose, gold and turquoise bands, never on a solid). Against helix: no stone, no tan; green that leans yellow and dark, not teal; lemon light from below instead of amber from lamps. Against burrow: no flesh, coral or magenta mass, and hard glossy surfaces. Against dollhouse: a dark sky over a bright floor, two saturated tones, not pastels in daylight. Against orbit: green where it is indigo, one glowing floor where it has gold points, no cyan.

## The measured curve (local frame, from `season(4)`; table plane at y = -9; 1 unit is about 1 cm)

Helix axis (-10.0, -7.7), radius 10.2. Camera: t=5 at the origin heading -Z (call it north); t=8 (-1, 0.2, -12) just starting to turn; t=10 north of the axis heading west, y 2.8; t=13 west of it heading south, y 7.1; t=16 south of it heading east, y 11.5; t=19 back over the start at (0, 13.7, -9) heading north; exit at (-5.2, 13.7, -31.4), flying 26 degrees left of north. Bank 16 degrees from t=8 to 18.

## The set

- **The flood.** The tray (radius about 95, scalloped rim) holds a shallow sheet of primrose tea that is the world's lamp. A dark lace doily lies in it under the teapot. Every object standing in it wears a bright meniscus collar climbing its base.
- **The teapot** on the helix axis: belly radius 7.3, lid at y 5.5, squat, so the upper half of the climb is open air. Its ear-shaped **handle** points east and the track threads it at t=6.6 (opening 5.7 wide, 7.5 tall). Its short curly **spout** points west (tip no more than 7.8 from the axis, y 3.5) and pours a rope of light **upward**, curving in to an upside-down cup at the tip of a curl of stacked cups that grows from the lid; the tea hangs under that cup as a glowing dome at y 11 to 13.
- **Curls.** Cups and saucers stacked so each stands on the one before, which makes the stacks arc over like fern heads. Five tall ones (14 to 26 units) to the west; one as the sleeper's pedestal; one cradling the straw's tip at the exit.
- **The sleeper**, dead ahead of the joint at about (0, y, -47): a hopper curled in a breakfast cup (radius 5.5, rim at y 1) on a curled pedestal, ears drooped over the rim, straw in the corner of its mouth.
- **The straw.** Glass, 0.9 thick, a vermilion spiral stripe. From the sleeper's mouth it droops east, runs south at x about +10 propped in the bowls of standing spoons, U-bends at z about +32, then runs 1.3 under the camera's whole track: approach, handle, helix, final approach, tip.
- **South:** the U-bend, bowed spoons, a causeway of sugar cubes. **East:** a milk jug pouring a white ribbon up into the sky.
- **Sky.** The inside of a soap-film dome sitting on the tray rim: primrose haze at the horizon, bottle green above, slow marbled sheen fed by the milk ribbon, a clear "black film" spot at the zenith where rising drops ring it.

## Opening silhouette (t=0 to 5)

The previous portal is a true window, so at t=0 it shows only the central 9 degrees of this camera's view, looking past the joint; the subject has to stand on that line. It is the sleeper: a cup on a curled stem with a round head and two long drooped ears, lit from below, straddling the horizon. Lower half of the stamp: primrose flood, the brightest and most yellow thing possible against indigo. Upper half: bottle green. Two straw lines converge on it from the bottom corners. Read: "someone with long ears asleep in an egg-cup, glowing from underneath." From t=3 the widening window brings in the pot's belly on the left and the handle ring ahead, which frames the sleeper at the takeover.

## Inhabitants

One instanced draw, about 18 hoppers plus the sleeper: a dumpling body (radius 2.6), two long ears, kangaroo feet, a tail with a pom, half-lidded bead eyes; bone-white fur, vermilion ear linings and cheeks. About 700 triangles each.

Motion from the clock. Each hopper has a ring of 3 to 5 **perches** (point and surface normal, stored in a small float `DataTexture`), a period T of 4.5 to 7 s and a phase. `n = floor((t + phase) / T)` picks perches A and B, `u = fract(...)` the stage: doze (ears sink, slow breathing squash) until 0.55, wake and crouch to 0.62, leap to 0.95, land with a squash. The leap is a cubic Bezier that leaves along A's normal and arrives along B's, and the body's up flips from one normal to the other at mid-flight: it falls toward whatever is nearest. Perches are only upward-facing or far-side surfaces, the shade, including the pot's flank and the underside of the dome's saucer. At mount, any leap that passes within 6 units of the track is dropped, so no cone push is needed for them. Rising drops (600 instanced quads on precomputed curves, `u = fract(t / T + phase)`) are pushed out of the camera cone as in `helix/koi.ts`.

The sleeper breathes on a 3.5 s cycle (2 s out, 1.5 s in). The same function drives the beads of tea in the straw (their offset along it is `1.25 t + 1.6 sin`, two steps forward and one back), the puff of its cheeks and the bubble.

## The exit

A soap bubble, radius 1.6, on the tip of the straw: smaller than a hopper's head, on a stem thinner than a finger, the thinnest thing in a world of thick porcelain.

- **Tip.** The straw's end is cut into five petals curled back like a flower, each still striped, with a gilt ferrule below and a bead of film in every notch. It rests in the top saucer of a curl that rises from the flood.
- **Bubble.** Two spheres. Inner: `portalMaterial`, centred 0.9 past `frame(exit)` on the tangent, so the screen is covered before the eye touches the film and the eye is inside at t=25. Outer: the film, Fresnel alpha, bands thickest at the top (films drain upward here), curved reflections of the world's real lights (the pour as a streak, the flood as a rim glow below), a collar where it meets the petals, a bead on top that lifts off every few seconds, two tiny bubbles stuck beside the tip.
- **Blown during the ride.** Until t=12 there is only a film bead across the petals (the portal sphere has zero scale, so nothing black shows). From 12 to 20 it swells a breath at a time, sagging a little on each in-breath; steady from 20.5.
- **Arrival.** The straw leads to it. It appears 16 degrees left at t=20 and centres by 23. From 23.5 to 24.3 the sheen thins to nothing, so the last frames are pure portal and the seam holds.

## How it is built

- **Up-light porcelain**, one material for all crockery: an upside-down hemisphere light (primrose from below, bottle green from above), analytic point lights at the pour, the dome, the sleeper's cup and the two nearest straw beads, an emissive "pool" on down-facing surfaces with a noisy edge, glaze specular, and the painted decoration (scallop band, dots, gilt rim, crackle) computed in the fragment shader from lathe coordinates so it holds 3 units from the lens.
- **Thin film**, one shader for the sky dome, the bubble and the notch beads: a thickness field through a three-cosine palette. The sky's field is baked to a 1024 x 512 texture at mount and animated by two scrolling samples.
- **Curl stacking**, one build function: each piece takes the frame of the previous piece's top turned by a small bend. It emits instance matrices, perches, and meniscus collars.
- Flood: one disc; lace baked from signed distance shapes to a 2048 texture; six analytic ripple rings.

## The kit, item by item

- **Singled:** one straw, one sleeper, one bubble; the ride is a single line from one to the other.
- **Slurping:** beads of tea jerk along the straw two steps forward, one back; tea climbs every base.
- **Sleepily:** one 3.5 s breath times the straw, the bubble and the hoppers' dozing.
- **Gravity points at whatever is nearest:** curled stacks, the upward pour into an upside-down cup, menisci, hoppers landing on any surface and flipping mid-leap.
- **Soap film:** the sky and the exit are the same film.
- **The size of a teacup:** the whole ride happens inside one tea tray; the lens passes 3 cm from the pot; the inhabitants fit in cups.
- **Light that falls upward:** the floor is the lamp, drops rise, light pools under overhangs, the sky is darkest at the top.
- **They hop between shadows:** the only shade is on top of things, so the hoppers treat the floor as lava.
- **Way in:** the spring curve is the straw: two straight ends and one loop, with the teapot placed inside the loop.

## Risks, and what to cut

1. **The bubble reads as a flat hole, or breaks the seam.** The portal is a screen-space window, so the roundness has to come from the film shell. Fallback: a flat film stretched across the splayed petals.
2. **Under-lighting turns eerie or muddy.** Faces lit from below can look sinister. Keep eyes closed in smiles, give fur a soft self-lit fill, hold the flood just under the bloom threshold, and judge the t=5 frame first.
3. **Clutter and clearance.** The handle fly-through and the pour pass 3 units from the eye, and the pot inside the loop must not read as the helix's column: keep it squat and verify clearances against `stretch.frame`.

Cut first: the milk jug and the sky's marbling (a plain film gradient survives); then the spoons and sugar causeway; then the lid curl and hanging dome (the pour then rises straight to the zenith).
